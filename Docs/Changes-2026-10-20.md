# 2026-10-20 (evening)

## Remote Play audio stutter
- The main-thread AVAudioSession warning was not the cause of the stutter. Session/engine work now runs on a dedicated serial queue anyway.
- Real causes: the jitter buffer was too small for Wi-Fi (40 ms start, trimmed back to 50 ms whenever it passed 120 ms), and gaps were hard cuts.
- New StreamAudioPlayer: 60 ms start threshold that adapts up to 160 ms after underruns and eases back after ~10 s stable; trims only past 300 ms (to 150 ms, whole frames); 2 ms fade in/out around gaps; 10 ms IO buffer; session activated once per stream.

## Always launch on Home
- `displayMode` is no longer restored from UserDefaults.
- Main WindowGroup: `.defaultLaunchBehavior(.presented)` and `.restorationBehavior(.disabled)`. ImmersiveSpace: `.restorationBehavior(.disabled)`.

## Bitrate
- Added a 5 Mbps option for weak Wi-Fi. Existing options: Automatic (chiaki preset), 10, 15, 20, 30 Mbps.

## Controller doesn't drive the UI while streaming
- `AppModel.gamepadEventsForUI` returns `.gamepad` while `activeSource == .remote`, otherwise `[]`.
- Applied with `.handlesGameControllerEvents(matching:)` (SwiftUI, visionOS 2+) on the root window content, the player ornament, the immersive RealityView and its control attachment. Gamepad events then go only to GameController, which keeps feeding the PS5. Home and capture mode keep normal controller navigation.

## Round 3 (evening)
- Player panels (Enhance, Resolution, Stream, Stats) are now sheets owned by PlayerView instead of popovers on the auto-hiding ornament. Fixes "Stream button works once, then nothing" (popover torn down by ornament hide, binding stuck at true). Auto-hide pauses while a panel is open.
- Stream settings available in the immersive theater (toolbar "Stream", segmented pickers) and in the theater window panel.
- "Apply Now (Reconnect)": restarts the session with new settings; waits 4 s and retries up to 2 times on error quit (PS5 replies InvalidSessionId while the old session closes). Stale events from a closed session are ignored (core identity token).
- Bitrate adds 40 / 50 Mbps (experimental). 1080p is the protocol max (chiaki presets 360/540/720/1080).
- Resolution picker is segmented. RemoteSettingsSections extracted for embedding in other Forms.
